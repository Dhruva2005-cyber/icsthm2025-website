About Us

The International Conference on Synergising Thought and Technology in Humanities and Management (ICSTHM) aims to explore how the intersection of humanistic insights and technological innovation is shaping the future of leadership, education, business, and society.

With experts from across the globe, ICSTHM 2025 promises deep academic discussions, industry collaborations, and impactful takeaways.
